Our Tours Plans

Dasaswamedh Ghat

Dashashwamedh Ghat Tour offers a mesmerizing spiritual experience on the banks of the Ganges in Varanasi. Famous for its vibrant Ganga Aarti, ancient legends, and sacred rituals, the ghat draws countless pilgrims and tourists seeking peace, devotion, and cultural beauty.

Dasaswamedh Ghat​

Vindhyachal Tour

Vindhyachal Tour offers a sacred journey to the revered Vindhyavasini Temple, nestled along the Ganges River. Known for its spiritual energy, beautiful ghats, and vibrant festivals, the destination attracts pilgrims seeking blessings, peace, and a divine cultural experience.

Vindhyachal Tour

Mahakaleshwar Ujjain

Mahakaleshwar Temple in Ujjain is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, renowned for its mystical Bhasma Aarti performed at dawn. The temple's spiritual aura, ancient heritage, and divine energy attract countless devotees seeking blessings and inner peace.

Mahakaleshwar Ujjain​

Vishwanath Temple

Vishwanath Temple in Varanasi, dedicated to Lord Shiva, is one of the twelve sacred Jyotirlingas. Known for its spiritual power and historical importance, the temple attracts millions of devotees seeking blessings, peace, and a deep connection to divine energy.

Vishwanath Temple

Sarnath

Sarnath Tour takes you to the sacred site where Lord Buddha gave his first sermon after enlightenment. Surrounded by ancient stupas, monasteries, and the iconic Ashokan Pillar, Sarnath offers a peaceful, historical, and spiritual experience for travelers and Buddhist pilgrims alike.

Sarnath

Kathmandu

Kathmandu Tour offers a vibrant blend of ancient temples, rich culture, and Himalayan beauty. Explore historic sites like Swayambhunath and Pashupatinath, bustling local markets, and serene mountain views, making it a perfect destination for spiritual seekers and adventure enthusiasts alike.

Kathmandu

Ayodhya

Ayodhya Tour takes you to the birthplace of Lord Ram, offering a divine experience through sacred temples, spiritual ghats, and Ram Janmabhoomi. Rich in history and devotion, Ayodhya is a revered destination for pilgrims seeking peace, faith, and cultural heritage.

Ayodhya

Pashupatinath Temple

Pashupatinath Temple in Kathmandu, Nepal, is one of the holiest shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. A UNESCO World Heritage Site, it’s known for its stunning architecture, spiritual atmosphere, sacred rituals, and importance in Hindu pilgrimage.

Pashupatinath Temple

About Us

Delivering Comfort, Safety, and Smart Travel Solutions

Kashi Royal Tours & Travels is your trusted partner for safe, smooth, and enjoyable travel experiences. Every vehicle in our fleet is fitted with GPS tracking and safety airbags, ensuring a secure and worry-free ride. Our team of professional drivers is known for their polite conduct and excellent road knowledge, making every trip comfortable and reliable.

We specialize in both city and outstation travel, offering well-maintained, spotless vehicles for every need. Whether you're heading to the airport, catching a train, or planning a longer journey, our airport pickup and drop services are available 24×7, along with round-the-clock transportation — all offered at pocket-friendly prices.

Choose from a wide range of cars including Honda City, Swift Dzire, Ertiga, Swift, Toyota Fortuner and a spacious 16-seater Tempo Traveller. With reasonable rental plans and experienced drivers who know every route, Kashi Royal Tours & Travels guarantees a stress-free and memorable travel experience from start to finish.
